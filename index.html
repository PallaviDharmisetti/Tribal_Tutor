About Tribal Tutors

We design learning paths that respect language, local knowledge and livelihood priorities. This platform helps learners who have limited connectivity or left school early, and connects them to verified tutors who can teach and earn.

Mission

Deliver education that fits local lives and leads to livelihoods.

How we work

Personalised curriculum, level-based tests, tutor verification and community support.

Impact

Fund learning kits, awareness programs and fair tutor payments via donations.
